# Brief

Peggy is a calendar, to-do and notes app for Android. One place to capture
anything in about two seconds without being asked to make decisions about it.
Local-first: no server, no analytics, no account.

Audience: people who have bounced off heavier task apps because capture costs
too much friction.

Three things a visitor must get in ten seconds:
1. It captures anything instantly, no forms, no required fields.
2. Calendar, tasks and notes are one surface, not three tabs.
3. Nothing leaves the phone.

The one action: join the closed test.

Taste direction: quiet and confident. Linear-grade typography restraint, not
gradient-SaaS. The screenshots are the hero; the copy gets out of their way.
Never more than one accent colour on screen at once.

Decided, do not re-litigate:
- No support email in the footer. The Play listing carries it.
- Cut the Pro tier claim from the FAQ entirely rather than publish an
  unconfirmed promise.
